and operational resilience.

## Expected Outcomes Achieved

-   Logs collected
-   Root Cause Analysis documented
-   Resolution steps validated

The simulated incidents demonstrate that the deployment platform can be
diagnosed, recovered, and returned to service using documented
operational procedures.
